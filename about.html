The Lawson Story

Victor Lawson spent years working as a licensed electrician under some of Hartford's largest contractors. He was good at the work — but frustrated watching homeowners get overcharged, talked down to, and left without clear answers about their own homes.

In 2010, Victor and his wife Karen made a decision: they would build the kind of electrical company they'd want to hire themselves. One where the estimate is honest, the technician shows up on time, and you get a full explanation of what was done and why.

Lawson Power Source started with Victor's truck, a few tools, and word of mouth from neighbors on Albany Ave. Fifteen years later, the business has grown to serve hundreds of Hartford-area homes — and the values haven't changed a bit.

Karen runs operations, client relations, and scheduling, making sure every customer interaction is as smooth as the electrical work itself. Together, they've built something the neighborhood trusts.

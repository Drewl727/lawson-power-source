What We Offer

Our Services

Expert electrical and home improvement services for Hartford-area homeowners and property managers — done right, every time.

Full-Service Electrical & Renovation

Everything We Do

Electrical Wiring & Repairs

Whether you're rewiring an older Hartford home, adding circuits for a new appliance, or troubleshooting outlets that stopped working — we diagnose and fix electrical problems correctly and to code. We don't guess; we test, verify, and document.

Panel Upgrades & Installations

An aging 60- or 100-amp panel can't keep up with today's homes. We upgrade panels to 150 or 200-amp service, install subpanels, and replace outdated fuse boxes with modern breaker panels — safely and with proper permitting.

Generator Installation

Connecticut storms can knock out power for days. We install Generac and other whole-home standby generators with automatic transfer switches, so your home keeps running when the grid doesn't — no extension cords required.

Bathroom & Kitchen Renovation

We handle full kitchen and bathroom remodels — from demo to finish. That includes tile, fixtures, vanities, cabinetry, and all the electrical rough-in and final connections. One crew, one contract, no coordination headaches.

Plumbing Rough-In & HVAC Work

New construction or gut renovation? We provide plumbing rough-in, drain and supply line work, and HVAC electrical connections. We coordinate with your general contractor or handle the full scope ourselves depending on project needs.

Property Management Services

We partner with Hartford-area landlords and property managers for ongoing electrical maintenance, unit turnover electrical work, code compliance upgrades, and multi-unit service agreements. Ask about our property management rates.

Emergency Electrical Services

Sparking outlets, burning smells, sudden power loss — these can't wait. Our emergency line is staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. When safety is at stake, we respond fast. No after-hours upsell; same honest pricing.
